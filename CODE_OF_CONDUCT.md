# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in the 16-bit CPU Simulator project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We aim to create a friendly environment where people can learn, ask questions, share ideas, review code, and contribute to the project constructively.

## Expected behavior

Project participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Provide constructive feedback.
- Accept different viewpoints and experiences.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.
- Give credit to others for their work.
- Respect project guidelines and maintainer decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting, or deliberately disruptive comments.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Threats of violence or encouragement of harmful behavior.
- Knowingly providing misleading information.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or educational community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews, and other official communication channels. It also applies when an individual is publicly representing the project or its community.

## Reporting unacceptable behavior

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainers through the contact method listed on the repository owner's GitHub profile.

Please include:

- A description of what happened
- The date and location of the incident
- The usernames or names of people involved
- Any relevant links, screenshots, or other evidence

Reports will be handled as confidentially as possible. Please do not create a public issue for sensitive reports.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may take any action they consider appropriate, including:

- Asking a participant to stop unacceptable behavior
- Removing or editing comments, issues, or other contributions
- Temporarily restricting participation
- Permanently restricting participation

Maintainers will act fairly, proportionately, and with consideration for the safety of everyone involved.
